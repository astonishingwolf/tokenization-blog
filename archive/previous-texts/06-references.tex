\section{References \& further reading}

\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09747}{FAST: Efficient Action Tokenization for
Vision-Language-Action Models} — the primary source for frequency-based action
tokenization and its reported results.

\href{https://huggingface.co/physical-intelligence/fast}{FAST official tokenizer
repository} — usage, chunk normalization, and encoding / decoding interface.

\href{https://github.com/openvla/openvla/blob/main/prismatic/vla/action_tokenizer.py}{OpenVLA
action tokenizer} — a concrete implementation of scalar action discretization.

\href{https://already-clean.goodfire.com/}{Neural Networks Are Already Clean} —
visual inspiration for this essay’s spacious, section-led presentation.
